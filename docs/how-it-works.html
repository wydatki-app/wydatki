How Wydatki works

From a bank file to the dashboard and your FIRE goal. Everything happens in your browser: files are read locally, and the page has network connections blocked (CSP connect-src 'none'). The short version: a 25-second animation.

automatic done by the appYou a decision or action by the user

1. Step by step

1

You download statements from your banks

You

OFX/QFX ("Download for Quicken") from any bank that offers it, including most US banks. Also supported: CSV from Millennium, mBank, PKO BP, Alior and Revolut; PDF "account/card history" from VeloBank; XLSX "operations statement" from BNP Paribas. Put them in the dane/ folder (dane = data) — preferably in per-bank subfolders (dane/ofx/, dane/mbank/, dane/velo/…), but that is only for your own tidiness: the app reads statements from the whole folder and detects the bank from the contents.

2

You open wydatki.html and pick the folder

You

Click "Choose your Wydatki folder" or drag the folder in. Folders starting with _ or . (e.g. _archive) are skipped. Files over 30 MB are skipped with a warning.

3

Bank detection

automatic

By content, not by name: the column header of a CSV or XLSX sheet (e.g. #Data operacji…#Kwota = mBank) or the PDF text ("VeloBank S.A."), and for OFX/QFX the OFXHEADER header. UTF-8 or Windows-1250 encoding is detected automatically. An unrecognized file → a warning in the top bar.

4

Reading and checking transactions

automatic
  • Date, description, payee, amount; for card payments — the actual purchase date.
  • Holds ("pending") are skipped — they come back as the final transaction.
  • A row with a bad date is rejected with a warning in the bar; a row without a valid amount is skipped (protection against a corrupted or planted file).
  • PDF and XLSX: limits on size (50 MB after unpacking) and complexity — a malicious file is skipped and the tab does not freeze. OFX/QFX: a file with DOCTYPE/ENTITY is rejected, limit of 100,000 transactions.
  • Main currency: from Settings, or the most common one in your files. Transactions in other currencies are skipped and reported in the bar — the app does not convert exchange rates and does not mix currencies in totals. Revolut: for foreign-currency accounts the equivalent in the main currency is used.
  • Source: the bank plus the card or account — last 4 digits only, the full number never leaves the parser.
  • Revolut: transfers to other people count as spending (with the bank fee); top-ups of the account do not.
5

Merging and removing duplicates

automatic

Exports with overlapping periods do not double your spending: a transaction with the same date, description and amount in several files counts once. Two identical transactions in one file (e.g. two $62 tickets on the same day) are two expenses. OFX/QFX: duplicates are recognized by the bank's transaction ID (FITID).

6

Exclusions

automatic

Own transfers, card repayments, transfers between your accounts and patterns from the _wyklucz section (wyklucz = exclude) are neither spending nor income — otherwise the same money would count twice.

7

Category, tags, income group

automatic

According to the rules — the order of decisions is in section 2 below. Tags from rules (e.g. "Kids" for a child's card), income gets a group (cashback, refunds, …).

8

You review and correct

You
  • Change a transaction's category → the app suggests a rule for similar (and future) transactions.
  • Manual and bulk tags (date range, e.g. "Vacation 2026"); switch off a rule's tag for a single transaction.
  • "To be refunded" — an expense you are waiting to get back disappears from the totals.
  • Monthly and annual budgets; the FIRE goal in the Settings view.
  • The Overview breakdown by bank and card: click a bank or card → Transactions with that filter; your own card names in Settings.
9

Cost-of-living layers and FIRE

automatic

Each expense lands in one layer (section 3). Current cost of living × 12 ÷ withdrawal rate = the capital needed for FIRE.

10

Saving your changes

automatic

Depending on the browser — straight to the folder, or in the browser with a backup reminder (section 4). The app never modifies your statements.

2. How a transaction gets its category

Checked from the top. The first match wins; lower levels are not reached.

0
Exclusion own transfer, card repayment, the _wyklucz section → the transaction stays out of the calculations
rule
1
Manual change on this transaction saved in override.json; applies to that one transaction only
You
2
Your rules from the app reguly_wlasne.json — a fragment of the description → a category, e.g. "kroger" → Groceries
You
3
Rules from the file kategorie.yaml from your folder, or the built-in defaults; the first matching category in file order
rule
4
No match "Other uncategorized" — worth checking after every import
default

Rules match the description and the payee, case-insensitive. In the app you type plain text; the full regular-expression syntax is available in kategorie.yaml (the "download kategorie.yaml to edit" button). Patterns that could freeze the app are rejected with a message. The default English categories (e.g. Groceries, Dining_out, Travel, Subscriptions, Family_and_gifts) and tags (e.g. Kids) are used until you provide your own file.

3. Cost-of-living layers (the FIRE card)

Each expense goes to the first matching layer. You set thresholds and tags in the Settings view (konfiguracja.json).

1category Traveltravel

Averages are computed from full months (the last 4 by default); the current month is shown with a forecast.

4. Where your changes are saved

Chrome, Edge

  1. You pick the folder with the button — the browser asks once for permission to write.
  2. Every change goes straight to a file in ustawienia/ (atomic write).
  3. Next time: "Open last folder".

Firefox, Safari, Brave

  1. Changes live in browser storage; the bar counts changes without a backup.
  2. After 10 changes or 3 days the bar turns urgent.
  3. "Save settings backup" → move the wydatki-ustawienia.json file into ustawienia/.
  4. On load the newer version wins: the backup in the browser or the files.
Shared computer: the "clear browser data" button removes the app's settings and the remembered folder. The files in your folder stay; if you have changes without a backup, the app warns you first.

5. Folder layout

Wydatki/ ├── wydatki.html ← the app (a single file) ├── dane/ ← statements (data); per-bank subfolders are optional (tidiness) │ ├── ofx/ ← OFX/QFX from any bank ("Download for Quicken") │ ├── millennium/ ← CSV "Historia transakcji" │ ├── mbank/ ← CSV "Elektroniczne zestawienie operacji" │ ├── pkobp/ ← CSV "Zestawienie operacji" │ ├── alior/ revolut/ ← CSV │ ├── velo/ ← PDF "Historia rachunku" / "Historia karty" │ └── bnp/ ← XLSX "Zestawienie operacji" (GOonline) └── ustawienia/ ← settings; created and filled in by the app ├── kategorie.yaml optional — rules from a file (without it: built-in) ├── reguly_wlasne.json rules added in the app ├── konfiguracja.json language, currency, FIRE goal, thresholds, kids and events tags, card names ├── override.json manual categories of single transactions ├── tagi.json zwroty.json budzety.json kategorie_wlasne.json

To start, wydatki.html and your statements are enough — all settings files are optional. The folder and file names are literal and stay in Polish.

6. Monthly routine (about 10 minutes)

  1. Download last month's statements and drop them into dane/ (e.g. dane/ofx/) (old files can stay — duplicates are not counted).
  2. Open the app and the folder; check the bar: number of files and transactions per bank, warnings.
  3. Look at "Other uncategorized" and fix categories — accept the rule suggestion so the same happens next month.
  4. Mark "to be refunded" and tag trips or events.
  5. Check the Budgets view and the FIRE card.
  6. Firefox, Safari, Brave: "save settings backup" and move the file into ustawienia/.
